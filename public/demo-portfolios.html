What is Analyst Upside?
Independent analysts at banks and research firms set price targets for stocks. The upside % is the gap between today's price and the consensus target — how much room analysts collectively think there is to grow.
📈
What does the performance chart show?
Each portfolio starts indexed at 100. The line shows how the basket of stocks and ETFs moved historically using official end-of-day closing prices. This is a historical look-back, not a forecast of future returns.
⭐
What is Conviction?
The filled dots show what share of covering analysts rate the instrument a Buy or Strong Buy. Five out of five means near-unanimous buy recommendation across all analysts following that stock or ETF.
